## Expert review list (agents never decide these)
**Patent lawyer**
- Whether the whitepaper, manuscript and README disclose inventions the Foundation intends to protect (public disclosure affects novelty).
- Third-party patent references or freedom-to-operate statements, if any appear in GFL or are imported from GeneForge.
- Contribution terms: whether a CLA or DCO is needed for an MIT project that the Foundation may build commercial products on.

**Regulatory expert**
- Whether any GFL component or doc (BioDecision gating, clinical neuro-skills, PharmGx, Geriatric Risk, NutriGx, "clinical-grade" representation) could qualify the software as a medical device under MDR rule 11, or an AI system under the AI Act, and what the not-for-clinical-use statement must say.
- Whether the genetic examples raise GDPR art. 9 obligations for users.
- ADNI/NACC content in `EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md`: DUA compliance for publishing variable dictionaries and derived transformations.
